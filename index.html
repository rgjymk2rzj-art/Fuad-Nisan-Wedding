Our theme

Yesterday, Today & Tomorrow

The Brunfelsia ‘yesterday, today, tomorrow’ flower was found growing upon the resting place of a family member who shaped, raised and inspired Nisan. Since then, the bloom has carried a quiet kind of meaning for the family — a reminder of those we honour yesterday, today and tomorrow.

Yesterday

The Brunfelsia opens in deep violet — for those who came before us, and the roots that hold us.

Today

It softens to lavender — for this season of love, faith and two families becoming one.

Tomorrow

And fades to white — a living portrait of life’s changing seasons, and the love that endures through them.
