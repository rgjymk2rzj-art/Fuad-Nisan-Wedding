  • Before you travel

    For most guests, entry to Kenya is through the Electronic Travel Authorisation (eTA). Requirements vary by nationality, so please check your eligibility and apply in good time.

    • Passport valid for at least 6 months
    • Have your itinerary and accommodation details ready
    • Plan airport pickup or first-night transport in advance
    • Allow extra time for Nairobi traffic

    Official Kenya eTA website · How to apply

    Some travellers, including certain East African nationals, may not require an eTA — please verify individually and rely on official government guidance.
