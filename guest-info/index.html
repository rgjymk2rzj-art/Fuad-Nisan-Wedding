Do I need a visa or eTA for Kenya?

For most guests, entry to Kenya is through the Electronic Travel Authorisation (eTA). Requirements vary by nationality, so please check your eligibility and apply in good time at etakenya.go.ke. Your passport should be valid for at least six months.

Some travellers, including certain East African nationals, may be exempt — please verify individually with official government sources.

Which airport should I fly into?

Jomo Kenyatta International Airport (NBO), Nairobi. Please plan your airport pickup or first-night transport in advance and allow extra time for traffic. Transfer providers →
